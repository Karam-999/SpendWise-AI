## FAQ (5 Real Q&As)

**Q: Is the SpendWise Audit actually free?**
A: Yes. No credit card, no trial, no catch. The audit runs entirely on deterministic rules with verified pricing data. We make money when high-savings users connect with our marketplace for discounted credits, not from the audit itself.

**Q: How accurate are the savings numbers?**
A: Every price in the engine traces back to an official vendor pricing page, verified within the current week. The logic is rule-based and deterministic, no AI-generated recommendations. A finance person can verify every number.

**Q: Do you store my data?**
A: Your form data stays in your browser via localStorage. Audit results are saved to our database only when you submit the form. The shareable URL strips identifying details like email and company name from the public version.

**Q: What is SpendWise / Credex?**
A: SpendWise is an AI spend optimization tool built by Credex. Credex sells discounted AI and cloud credits sourced from companies that overforecasted or pivoted. The discount is real, typically 45-50% off retail, and the credits are verified and fully transferable.

**Q: Why would I book a consultation?**
A: If your audit shows >$500/month in potential savings, a SpendWise advisor can help you capture those savings through discounted credits. No obligation, the consultation is free and takes 15 minutes.
